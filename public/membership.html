1. Code of Civil Conduct
All members of the Miritini Response Team (MRT) must uphold the highest standards of integrity, respect, and non-discrimination during public deployments, drills, and humanitarian emergency exercises.

Volunteer deployment during incidents (fires, sanitation drives, flood mitigation) is voluntary but must strictly follow the operational commands set by the Authorized Office Desk.
